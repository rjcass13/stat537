\section{Data Generation}

2 Treatments: Tmt1: 50, Tmt2: 55, or both are 50 (will do 1000 sets when equal, and 1000 when not equal)

10 Subjects per Treatments

5 Reps per Subject

100 data points


AR1 Covariance: $\sigma^2_e \begin{pmatrix}
    1 & \rho & \rho^2 & \rho^3 ... \\ 
    \rho & 1 & \rho & \rho^2 ... \\
    \rho^2 & \rho & 1 & \rho ...
\end{pmatrix}$

4 covariance structures (the following are the diagonal components):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Indepdenent: $\sigma^2_e I$
    \item CompSymm: $\sigma^2_e = 10, \sigma^2_s = 12$
    \item AR1: $\sigma^2_e = 10, \rho = .8: \begin{pmatrix}
        10 & 8 & 6.4 & 5.12 & 4.1 \\
        8 & 10 & 8 & 6.4 & 5.12 \\
        6.4 & 8 & 10 & 8 & 6.4 \\
        5.12 & 6.4 & 8 & 10 & 8 \\
        4.1 & 5.12 & 6.4 & 8 & 10 \\
    \end{pmatrix}$
    \item Unstructured: $\begin{pmatrix}
        15 & 10 & 12 & 12 & 2 \\
        10 & 18 & 5 & 13 & 8 \\
        12 & 5 & 20 & 5 & 3 \\
        12 & 13 & 5 & 15 & 10 \\
        2 & 8 & 3 & 10 & 12
    \end{pmatrix}$
\end{itemize}


Test using AIC/BIC (groups will pick one). Pick a covariance structure.

Generate data using each of the covariance structures. Then test each dataset using all 4 covariances. Then use the AIC/BIC to pick one.

Repeat this process 1000 times. In each set of 1000, do 500 with all the data, then 500 where we throw out 20\% of the data.
